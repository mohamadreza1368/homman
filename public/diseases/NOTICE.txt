The mondo-index.json file is a modified extract: it retains Mondo identifiers,
English labels and exact synonyms; obsolete terms and terms marked not_a_disease
are excluded. The original release and complete ontology remain available above.
definitions.json also contains English definitions and source references from
the same release and from Disease Ontology, as described below.
This index is for terminology search; it is not a diagnosis, a complete list
of all possible diseases, or an anatomical mapping.

do-sites.json is a modified extract of direct disease-has-location assertions
and inherited superclass assertions, with automatic exact-label atlas mappings
and a small number of explicit paired-organ mappings. These are unreviewed
educational associations; paired mappings do not indicate patient laterality.
